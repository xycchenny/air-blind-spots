About these numbers. Monitor locations and yearly averages come from the 876 monitors in our COGS 137 case study dataset (2008, lower 48 states and DC). That dataset may not include every monitor operating in 2008. Distances are straight-line miles from each county's geographic center to the nearest monitor in the dataset, so people at a county's edge may live closer or farther. Limits shown: U.S. EPA annual standard of 9 µg/m³ (2024) and WHO guideline of 5 µg/m³ (2021). This is not live air quality.
